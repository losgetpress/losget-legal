Scan This Food — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11/2/2026

Scan This Food is provided by Losget Press (“we,” “us”). This policy explains what information the App handles, what stays on your phone, and what leaves it.

1. Summary

  • The App has no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • Your family members’ names and pictures, and your Nutrition Watch and allergy settings, stay on your phone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • The App contains no advertising, and we do not operate usage analytics or crash-reporting services. Google’s ML Kit, which the App uses to read barcodes and text, may send Google certain diagnostic and usage information, as described in section 3.
  • We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We handle limited subscription, referral and report information, and some third-party services receive information, as described in section 3.

2. Information that stays on your phone

  • Family members
    The optional names and pictures you set for up to six family members.
  • Nutrition Watch and allergy settings
    The items and allergens you choose for each family member.
  • Camera images
    The camera is used to read barcodes and package labels. Images are processed on your phone, in memory, and are not saved or sent anywhere.
  • Label scan values
    The values read from a label you scan.
  • Product data and settings
    Product information the App has looked up, your language and theme choices, the time of your last product lookup, and the result of the last subscription check.

This information is excluded from Android’s automatic backup and device-to-device transfer. You can delete it at any time by clearing the App’s data in your phone’s settings or by uninstalling the App.

3. Information that leaves your phone

Open Food Facts
The App carries a copy of the USDA FoodData Central product data and looks products up on your phone. When a product is not found there, the App sends the barcode to Open Food Facts to look it up. As with any internet request, Open Food Facts receives your IP address. See the Open Food Facts privacy policy at https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy.

Google Play
Subscriptions are bought and managed through Google Play. The App asks Google Play for the price, starts a purchase or a restore when you ask for it, and checks whether a newer version of the App is available. Google Play handles purchases, subscription management, restoration and update checks under Google’s own terms and privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not receive your payment card details.

Subscription check
To confirm that a subscription is valid, the App sends the purchase token issued by Google Play to our server. A purchase token is a random string created by Google Play. It does not itself contain your name or email address, but it is linked to a subscription. Our server asks Google Play for the status and end date of the subscription. We use the purchase token only to check the subscription and to apply referral rewards, as described below.

Referral codes
Every subscriber receives a referral code automatically. While your subscription is active, our server keeps your code, your purchase token and the status of each referral, so that it can apply your reward when someone subscribes with your code. When your subscription ends, the server deletes your purchase token and keeps only the code and an “expired” status, until you ask us to delete them (section 7) or they are no longer needed to run the referral program. If you enter someone else’s referral code when you subscribe, the server records that your purchase used that code. These records contain no name, email address or device identifier.

Reports you send
If you use Report a Problem, the App sends us the problem type you choose, any details you write, the App version, App language, Android version and phone model, and, if you leave it selected, the barcode of the product you scanned last. Please do not include personal or health information in a report; if you include it anyway, we will receive it as part of the report. We use reports only to investigate problems and improve the App. We keep each report for no more than 24 months and then delete it, and we delete a report sooner if you ask (section 7).

Barcode and text recognition
The App uses Google’s ML Kit to read barcodes and text. Camera images are processed on your phone and are not sent to Google by ML Kit. Google states that ML Kit may send Google device and app information, installation or device identifiers, and diagnostic and usage information; see https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms and https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure.

Our server runs on Google Cloud in the United States. Like any server, it receives the IP address of each request in order to answer it. We do not use IP addresses to identify you, and we do not store them in our subscription, referral or report records. Google Cloud may process request information as part of providing its services, under its own terms.

In short: Google Play (subscriptions and update checks), Google ML Kit (barcode and text recognition on your phone) and Open Food Facts (lookups of products not in the App’s USDA data) may receive the limited information described above. We do not receive the family names, pictures, Nutrition Watch settings, allergy settings or camera images stored or processed on your phone.

4. Permissions

The App uses four Android permissions: camera (to scan), internet and network state (to look up products and subscriptions), and Google Play billing (to subscribe). It does not request your location, contacts, microphone, photos or storage. When you choose a picture for a family member, Android’s own picker gives the App only the picture you select.

5. Health-related settings

Your Nutrition Watch and allergy settings may relate to health. They are stored only on your phone, are used only on your phone to produce ratings and alerts, and are never sent to us or to third parties.

6. Age

Only adults aged 18 or older may use the App. The App is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. “Kids’ Nutrition” is a setting for adults who choose food for children; it does not mean that children may use the App.

7. Your choices and rights

The App has no accounts, and we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number. You can delete everything stored on your phone as described in section 2. To ask us to delete a report or the record of your referral code, email contact@losget.com. Give the date of the report and a short description, or the referral code; please do not send the content of a report again unless it is needed. We may ask for limited additional information to confirm that the record is yours, and we will respond as required by applicable law. We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We will not treat you differently for exercising rights you have under the law of your state.

8. Security

Information sent by the App travels over encrypted connections. Access to our server is limited to the people who operate the App.

9. Changes

When this policy changes, we will post the new version in the App and at https://losgetpress.github.io/losget-legal/scan-this-food/privacy/ with a new effective date. If a change is material, the App will tell you before it takes effect.
