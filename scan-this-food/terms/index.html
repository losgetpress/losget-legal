2. What the App does

The App reads barcodes and package labels of packaged foods sold in the United States. It shows ratings, generated by the App from public information from the FDA and the Dietary Guidelines for Americans, according to the Nutrition Watch items you select. It checks the ingredient text available to it for the allergens and sulfites you select. It does not test food, and it cannot confirm that a food is free of allergens, sulfites or cross-contact.

4. Allergens: always read the package

The App is an aid. It does not replace reading the package. Product data comes from databases and from what your camera reads; it can be incomplete, out of date or wrong, and manufacturers change their products. “Not listed” means only that an allergen was not found in the ingredient text available to the App; it does not mean the product is free of that allergen or safe for you. If there is any discrepancy, the original package prevails. Before eating or serving a product, check the actual package, including the ingredient list, any “Contains” statement and any other allergy warning. If you or a family member has a food allergy, read the package label every time, and do not rely on the App alone.
