How the Score Works

Delivery & Ride Quick Offer

This page explains how this app decides whether to report an Offer and how it calculates the score from 1 to 10. The same Offer with the same settings always gets the same result. All processing and recognition happen on your phone. This app only reports; the final decision to accept is yours.

1. What the score is based on

When a platform app sends you an Offer, this app reads some of its contents, such as the amount, the distance, the time and the number of items, and compares them with the conditions you have preset in Offer Rules.

This app supports five platforms: Uber, Uber Eats, DoorDash, Instacart Shopper and Walmart Spark. Because each platform works differently, you can preset different monitoring conditions for each one. The score is calculated in the same way for all five.

Information that an Offer does not contain is left out of recognition and reporting. This app never fills in a value of its own.

2. Step one: your conditions

In Offer Rules you can set a value for each condition. An Offer that fails to meet any one of these values is not reported: no voice and no popup.

The conditions that apply depend on the platform:

  • Uber: minimum Offer amount, minimum dollars per mile, minimum dollars per hour, maximum total distance, maximum pickup distance, maximum number of stops, excluded areas.
  • Uber Eats: minimum Offer amount, minimum dollars per mile, minimum dollars per hour, maximum total distance, maximum number of stops, excluded areas.
  • DoorDash: minimum Offer amount, minimum dollars per mile, minimum dollars per hour, maximum total distance, maximum number of stops, order types you do not take, excluded areas.
  • Instacart Shopper: minimum Offer amount, minimum dollars per mile, maximum total distance, maximum number of items, minimum dollars per item, maximum number of stops, order types you do not take, excluded areas.
  • Walmart Spark: minimum Offer amount, minimum dollars per mile, maximum total distance, maximum number of items, minimum dollars per item, maximum number of stops, order types you do not take, excluded areas.

A condition is checked only when the Offer contains the information it needs. When the Offer has no amount, it has no score and is reported as "score unavailable".

3. Step two: the score from 1 to 10

An Offer that meets all your conditions gets a score. The score shows how far the Offer is above your minimums.

  • Each available measure is rated from 1 to 10: Offer amount, dollars per mile, dollars per hour, dollars per item, according to the platform's set of conditions.
  • A measure exactly at your minimum is rated 1. A measure at 1.5 times your minimum, or higher, is rated 10. In between, the rating rises evenly.
  • The score is the average of these ratings, rounded to a whole number.
  • An Offer whose pickup or drop-off area matches one of your preferred areas gets 1 extra point. The score never goes above 10.

Example: your minimum Offer amount is $8.00 and your minimum is $1.50 per mile. An Offer of $10.00 for 5 miles is $2.00 per mile. The amount is rated 5.5, because $10.00 is halfway between $8.00 and $12.00. The rate per mile is rated 7. The average is 6.25, so the score is 6. It is shown as 6/10.

4. What the score means

  • 8 to 10: reported as a Priority Offer.
  • 1 to 7: reported as an Offer that meets your conditions.
  • No score: the notification did not contain enough information.

Every Offer that has a score meets all your conditions. A score of 1 means the Offer is close to the minimum values of your conditions.

5. Places you have marked

  • Rejected place: an Offer whose pickup or drop-off falls inside a place you marked as rejected is treated like an Offer that does not meet your conditions. It is not reported.
  • Warning place: the Offer is scored as usual, and your warning and your reason are also spoken.
  • Rated place: a place you rated 5 adds 1 point to the score; a place you rated 1 takes 1 point away. The score stays between 1 and 10.

Your marks are stored only on your phone.

6. The setting "Ignore offers scoring below"

When you choose a value for this setting (5, 6, 7, 8 or 9), Offers that score below it, and Offers without a score, are not reported. When the setting is off, every Offer that meets your conditions is reported.

7. The figures in a report

  • Dollars per mile is the Offer amount divided by the distance.
  • Estimated dollars per hour is the Offer amount divided by the estimated time in the notification, for example "About $23.40/hr".
  • Dollars per item is the Offer amount divided by the number of items.

These estimates are all based on the notification contents of the Offer. They are not a promise of earnings.

8. Limits of this method

  • The score depends on the platform's notification. A report can be incomplete, late or different from what the platform app shows. For any Offer, what the app that sent it shows is the final authority.
  • The score only compares the information shown in the Offer with your preset conditions. It does not judge traffic, waiting time, tips added later or anything else that the notification does not state.
  • This app will not accept or decline an Offer for you. The decision is always yours.
