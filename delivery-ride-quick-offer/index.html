Delivery & Ride Quick Offer

The App is an information tool for delivery and rideshare drivers. When a delivery or ride platform app on your phone shows you a new offer, the App reads the notification that the platform app posts on your device, compares the offer with the rules you have set, and shows you a score, a short popup and, if you choose, a spoken report.

The App only reads and reports. It never accepts, declines or changes an offer for you. You make every decision yourself, and you tap the buttons in the platform's own app yourself.
